# 3D Printing: Check Your Knowledge

You've read the whole 3D Printing badge. Before you take the Canvas quiz, see what stuck. **Think through each answer first**, then click to check it. If you miss one, go back and reread that part before taking the quiz.

*These are practice, not graded.*

**1. You're printing an L-shaped phone stand. Should it stand up tall on the plate, or lie on its long flat side?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **Lie it on its long flat side.** A big flat face on the plate sticks well and needs few or no supports. Standing it up would need lots of supports.

**2. In the slicer preview, part of your model is floating in mid-air with nothing under it. What should you do before printing?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **Turn on supports or reorient the part.** Plastic can't print on thin air. If you print it like that, it will likely turn into a tangled "spaghetti" mess.

**3. You designed a lid exactly the same size as the opening on your box. What will probably happen?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **It won't fit.** Plastic shrinks a little as it cools, so openings print a bit small. Leave a gap (start around 0.2–0.3 mm) and print a small test fit first.

**4. Your print just finished. Why should you wait a bit before pulling it off the plate?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **It's hot, and cooled parts come off easier.** The plate can be over 100 °C. Let it cool, then flex the plate gently to pop the part off.

**5. Where should you do your Onshape modeling for a print?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **Before you come to the printer, on any computer.** Engineering laptops are in Cascade 112. Don't model on the printer's computer, because someone else may need it.

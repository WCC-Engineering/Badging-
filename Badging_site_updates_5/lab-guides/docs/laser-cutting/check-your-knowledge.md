# Laser Cutting: Check Your Knowledge

You've read the whole Laser Cutting badge. Before you take the Canvas quiz, see what stuck. **Think through each answer first**, then click to check it. If you miss one, go back and reread that part before taking the quiz.

*These are practice, not graded.*

**1. You designed a box whose sides meet at angled, beveled edges. Will the laser cut those angles?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **No.** The laser only cuts straight down, so every edge comes out at 90° to the sheet. Redesign it with flat parts that slot together, like finger joints.

**2. You open your DXF in CorelDRAW and every line is black. What happens if you send it to the laser like that?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **Nothing gets cut.** Black means raster engrave, so the laser would engrave instead of cutting. Select the lines you want cut and **right-click red**.

**3. A friend gives you a piece of plastic from home and asks you to cut it for them. What do you do?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **Don't cut it yet.** Any material that isn't provided has to be approved by Jonathan or an instructor first. Some plastics look harmless but release toxic gas or catch fire in the laser.

**4. Your Onshape drawing still has its border and title block. Why is that a problem?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **The laser cuts every line in the file**, including the border and title block. A laser cut drawing should be a blank sheet with only the outlines of your parts.

**5. Your job is running and you remember something you left in another room. What do you do?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **Stay in the room until it's done.** Fire is the biggest risk with the laser, and someone has to be there to press pause and get help if a flame keeps burning.

# Lab Rules

These rules apply to every machine in the WCC Engineering Lab.

## Before you use any machine

- I've completed the badge for this machine.
- I've passed the safety quiz in Canvas.
- My design/file is ready **before** I sit down at the lab computer.

## Always

- **Stay with the machine.** Never leave a running laser cutter or a hot soldering iron unattended.
- **Wear safety glasses** when soldering, trimming leads, or removing supports.
- **Ventilation on.** Laser ventilation fan or soldering fume absorber, every time.
- **Ask when unsure.** Asking a question is a skill, not a weakness.
- **Report problems right away.** Weird noises, smells, error messages, or damaged equipment.
- **Clean up.** Leave the station the way you'd want to find it.

## Never

- Never put an unknown material in the laser cutter.
- Never open the laser cutter lid while it's running.
- Never catch a falling soldering iron. Let it drop and step back.
- Never eat or drink at a soldering station.

## In an emergency

- **Fire in the laser:** press pause on the control panel, keep the lid closed, and get the instructor.
- **Burn:** cool running water right away, then tell the instructor.
- Know where the **fire extinguisher** and **first aid kit** are before you start.

# What Is a Badge?

A **badge** is a lab guide with step-by-step instructions that teaches you how to use a machine or process in the WCC Engineering Lab. Each badge covers one machine or process, so you can learn them one at a time.

## Badges you can earn

| Badge | What you'll learn |
|---|---|
| :material-printer-3d: [**3D Printing**](3d-printing/index.md) | Turn an Onshape part into a printed object on the Bambu Lab X1-Carbon |
| :material-laser-pointer: [**Laser Cutting**](laser-cutting/index.md) | Turn an Onshape drawing into a laser-cut part on the Trotec Rayjet 300 |
| :material-soldering-iron: [**Soldering**](soldering/index.md) | Make electrical connections that last by building a flashlight kit |

New to CAD? Start with [**Onshape**](onshape/index.md). Every machine here starts from an Onshape design.

## How to earn a badge

1. **Work through every page of the badge, in order**, before you come to lab. Take your time. This is where you learn the process.
2. **Finish with Check Your Knowledge.** Practice questions show you what stuck and what to reread.
3. **Take the badge's safety quiz in [Canvas](https://wcc.instructure.com/courses/2771437).** You need 100% to pass, and you can retake it as many times as you need.
4. **Bring the Step-by-Step pages to lab** on your phone or laptop and follow along.

!!! tip "Stuck?"
    Find Jonathan, any of the peer mentors, or an instructor to help! See [Ask for Help](help.md).

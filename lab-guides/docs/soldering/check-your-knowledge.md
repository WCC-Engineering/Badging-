# Soldering: Check Your Knowledge

You've read the whole Soldering badge. Before you take the Canvas quiz, see what stuck. **Think through each answer first**, then click to check it. If you miss one, go back and reread that part before taking the quiz.

*These are practice, not graded.*

**1. You melt solder onto the iron's tip, then try to dab it onto the joint, and it just balls up. What went wrong?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **You heated the solder instead of the joint.** Touch the iron to the pad and the lead together first, then feed solder to the joint on the side opposite the iron.

**2. Your iron is hot, but solder won't flow off the tip. What should you check?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **The tip is probably oxidized.** Wipe it on the damp sponge or brass cleaner until it's shiny, then re-tin it with a little fresh solder.

**3. You've held the iron on one joint for over 10 seconds, waiting for it to look right. What's the risk?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **You could lift the copper pad off the board.** Aim for about 3–5 seconds. If it's not working, pull the iron away, let it cool, add flux, and try again.

**4. You finish your flashlight, but the LED doesn't light up. What's one thing to check first?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **Which way the LED is facing.** LEDs only work one way: the long leg is positive (+) and the short leg is negative (−).

**5. You're done soldering for the day. What do you do with your station?**

??? success "Show answer"
    **Tin the tip, turn off the iron, and leave it in the stand to cool.** Then turn off the fume absorber, clean up clipped leads, put tools back, and wash your hands.

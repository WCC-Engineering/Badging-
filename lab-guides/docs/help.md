Stuck? Not sure about a step? **Asking is a skill engineers use every day.** Here's who can help, and how to reach them.

!!! danger "Emergency or something unsafe?"
    Stop the machine (pause button on the laser, or turn off the iron), step back, and get the **nearest instructor or lab staff right away**.
    For a medical emergency or fire, call **911**, then campus security: **[CAMPUS SECURITY PHONE]**.

## Which person should I ask?

| If you need… | Ask |
|---|---|
| Help following a guide, or a second pair of eyes on your file | **Peer mentors** |
| A machine isn't working, or you need materials | **Jonathan** |
| Training sign-off before using a machine | **Jonathan** or **your instructor** |
| Questions about an assignment or grade | **Your instructor** |
| Something unsafe is happening | **Anyone nearby. Right away.** |

## Before you ask, it helps to have…

- [ ] **Which machine** and **which step** you're on (the page name helps!)
- [ ] **What you expected** and **what happened instead**
- [ ] A **photo** of the problem or error message, if you can

!!! tip "No question is too small"
    Everyone who uses these machines started as a beginner. Asking early saves your project, your time, and the machine.
